**The stores follow the core's 0.1.1 contracts** (agbossjs/agboss#1321, #1317, #1319, #1320, #1341, #1307, #1310): the baseline schema (version 1) grows the fields, collection and indexes below — no migration of a 0.1.0 database. The stores import their contracts from `@agboss/core/stores`. `MongoFencedEffectRepository` extends the core's `FencedEffectRepository` (no settings, no transaction argument): the work is handed no transaction and writes through `connection.requireTransaction()` (or `requireMongoTransaction()`); results, a scope's mark and a business key's lock carry `expiresAt` (the mark and the lock keep the latest of their keys, `null` over any date) and `deleteExpired(now)` removes what is due; a business key is looked up under the previous secret's digest too. `MongoOutboundCallRepository` takes the `expiresAt` of `markCompleted` / `markFailed`, pages as `Page<OutboundCallRecord>` (`cursor`, `nextCursor`) and sweeps with `deleteExpired(now)`; a refused write is `ExecutionError.Fenced` (`fencedRefusalOf`). `deleteExpired` replaces the `delete…Before` retention methods of the audit log (an ended tree's events only), the history, the mesh requests, the A2A tasks (per endpoint's cutoff) and the direct messages (per endpoint, completed before the cutoff). The A2A tasks, messages and direct messages carry their `endpoint` (indexes `by_endpoint`) and `cancelRequestedAt` reads back as a `Date`. Mesh requests are held by an attempt under a lease (`attemptId`, `lockedUntil`, `renewLease`, fenced `saveResponse` / `deleteByKey`, takeover of a lapsed lease by the same request, `deleteExpired` by `expiresAt`). The cost ledger counts tokens (`findUsageByCounter`, `findLimitByAccount`, `saveLimit`); the child queue keeps `placement` and `excludeInstanceId` (`findQueuedIsolatedWorkerChildren`, `claimIsolatedWorkerByTenantIdAndExecutionId`, `withdrawByTenantIdAndExecutionIdIfQueued`); records and summaries name their `workflow` and `memory`, the history's period listings page by position. A reassigned pause's replacement is made as the core makes it (`assignees`, `actor`, `reason`, an expiry's `due`, a `reassigned` entry in its trail); a decided-on-expiry answer has no `actor`.
